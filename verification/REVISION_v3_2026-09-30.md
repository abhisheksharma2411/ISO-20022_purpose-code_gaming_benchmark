# Revision v3 report, 30 September 2026

Second-round review (external, on the v2 manuscript) asked for experimental
and domain validity rather than prose: five pages, corrected Swift statement,
equation/code agreement for splitting, a temporal pair-aware protocol,
paired counterfactual SEL, a formal contradiction-versus-family test, XSD and
invariant validation, prevalence and generator-shift robustness, and a
history-aware positive control. All were implemented; the harness was
rewritten (commit `45149f3`, pre-registration in `analysis/`), the protocol
run at commit `17bb868` (ten seeds x 200,000 records, 37 minutes), and the
paper regenerated from `results/summary.json`.

The claim that an internal contradiction predicts detectability, which the v2
paper presented as its central empirical finding, was written down as a
hypothesis before the v3 run and failed its test. The paper now reports the
null result and the reason (P2c omission is highly recovered though
"coherent"; P1b contradiction is barely recovered because no feature reads
it) and replaces the claim with the family-level evidence result from
leave-one-family-out: P3 is caught by prior pair history and by nothing
message-level.
