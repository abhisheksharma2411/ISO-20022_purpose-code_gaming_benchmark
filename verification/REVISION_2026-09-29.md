## Defects found and fixed before any prose was changed

| Defect | Where | Effect measured | Fix | Commit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Supervised detector scored on its own training rows (fit on even rows, scored on all rows; metrics over all rows) | `detector_scores`, `evaluate` | +0.03 AUC at 50k rows (0.964 on training rows vs 0.902 held out); matched in-distribution LOO baseline likewise in-sample | two-fold cross-fitting by row parity for every learned model | `ab1c59c` |
| C5 pair history excluded gamed messages, i.e. used the label, and counted a benign row's own purpose | `build_history`, `features` | label leak into a feature used by D3/D4/D5 | history from the other fold only, no labels | `ab1c59c`, `d724ade` |
| D4 standardised with whole-corpus statistics | `detector_scores` | small; disclosed and fixed | other-fold statistics | `d724ade` |
| Benign remittance text had exactly two lengths (9, 22 chars); benign names fixed format; benign batches stopped at 49 while P3b reached 200 | generator | a one-line out-of-range rule recovered 19.96% at 1% capacity (ceiling 20.1%) with zero benign alerts; 100% of D2/D6 alert mass and 96.5% of D5's sat on out-of-range rows | variable-length text and names, benign batch tail to 200; audit kept as a regression check | `378369d` |

After the fixes: 1.1% of gamed records are out of range, no benign record is,
and at most 5% of any detector's alert mass at 1% capacity falls on them.

Conclusions that survived: recovery at a small capacity is a fraction of AUC's
promise; the supervised model collapses on an unseen family; P3 is near chance
for every message-level detector; detectability follows internal
contradiction rather than the P1/P2/P3 family. New: the label-free AUC order
(D5 > D4 > D1) is the reverse of the recovery order; the below-chance held-out
P1 score is a systematic inversion caused by the ordinary-error floor.
