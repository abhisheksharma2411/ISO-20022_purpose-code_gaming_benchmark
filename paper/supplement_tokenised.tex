\section{Features and detectors}
Message-level vector (11): C1--C4, C5 (purpose novelty against prior pair
history), purpose absent, remittance length, address-line count, child
count, ultimate absent, any party name of at most five characters. History
vector (6 beyond C5): path novelty (share of prior messages on the other
path), split deviation ($|\log n_{\mathrm{ch}}-\text{mean}|/(\text{s.d.}+0.5)$
over prior files, 0 with fewer than three), ultimate-name novelty,
ultimate-presence deviation, amount deviation (same form on $\log(1+a)$),
purpose never seen before (with at least three prior messages). Raw vector
(26): one-hot purpose (9 incl. absent), category (6), corridor (6),
$\log(1+a)$, child count, ultimate absent, town absent, remittance length.
$D_0=\mathrm{C1}+\mathbb{1}[\mathrm{C2}>1]$; $D_1$ as in the paper; $D_2$
scikit-learn \texttt{HistGradientBoostingClassifier}, 150 iterations, other
settings default; $D_3$ \texttt{IsolationForest}, 200 trees, on benign
training rows; $D_4=z(D_1)+z(D_3)$ with training-window statistics; $D_5$
\texttt{LocalOutlierFactor}, 20 neighbours, novelty mode, standardised benign
training rows; $D_6$ $\ell_2$ logistic regression on the standardised raw
vector; $D_7$ sum of the six history deviations standardised on the training
window. No class weighting, no tuning, no validation-set selection other
than the alert threshold.

\section{Ethics and release}
None of the eleven operations is disclosed here for the first time; code
lists, the harmonised data requirements, and the published screening
principles are public, and market-practice guidance already records purpose
codes placed in the wrong field. The asymmetry favours the defender, who can
map the families onto screened fields, paths, and history features without
disclosing payment data. The release is non-operational: all weights are
illustrative constants, no value is calibrated against a real corridor or
deployment, and the corpus contains no customer, employer, or production
alert data.

\section{Domain-rater questionnaire}
For each primitive (definitions only, blind to any result), rate 1--5 with
one sentence of reasoning: Q1 actor plausibility; Q2 semantics preservation
(same amount, same economic originator and beneficiary); Q3 cost ordering;
Q4 schema plausibility; Q5 already described in public guidance; Q6 (yes/no)
does the edit make two elements of the message, or an element and the
creditor's profile, inconsistent. Two or three raters with payment-message
or financial-crime-control experience; report Cohen's $\kappa$ for Q6 and
Kendall's $W$ for Q1--Q5; Q6 answers replace the pre-specified labels and
the contradiction test is rerun unchanged.
